# Workday: site notes for filling a form

Read by the applier, and by a session that fills this site's form, with `../filling.md`.

- Workday can end the session
  after about 25 minutes on one application: save each step as soon as it is filled, and check that the session is
  still signed in before the next step.
- Never reload a Workday page: a reload can sign the tab out, and the saved draft then keeps only steps 1 and 2.
  A stuck page: the form's own Back and Next. Signed out anyway: the person signs in, then steps 3 onwards are
  filled again.
- Workday's choice to go on without AI help is refused by the browser tool's permission check: hand that field to
  the person at once (the tab held and titled), never another way round.
