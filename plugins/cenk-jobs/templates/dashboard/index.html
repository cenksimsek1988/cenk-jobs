Urgent first: do it now, it expires (a code, a CAPTCHA, a deadline within 24 hours). Then Answer first: one row per session that waits on you, numbered in the order to answer them, with its applications; a one-line answer can go to your main session. Then in progress: Claude, or a session with you, is on it, so wait for it. Then everything waiting on you, deadlines first (a past one on top: tell Claude whether it was done), then the longest-waiting first: sign-ins to click, questions only you can answer, and tasks such as online tests or AI interviews. When one is done, tell Claude. Last, Claude's next: applications that wait on nobody but Claude. A wait on an employer is not here: see Hiring steps.
